I’m new to training. Where do I start?+

Tell us what you’d like to work on and your current experience. Ask about beginner-friendly options before choosing a session.

Can I ask about personal coaching?+

Yes. Select “Personal coaching” in the enquiry form and let us know what kind of support you’re looking for. Confirm availability directly with the team.

Where can I find prices and session times?+

Contact us on Instagram or Facebook for current fees, location and availability before making plans to visit.

What happens when I prepare an enquiry?+

You’ll get an editable message to copy and send on Instagram or Facebook. A session is confirmed only after you’ve heard back from the team.
