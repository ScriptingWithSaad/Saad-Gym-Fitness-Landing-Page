# Saad Gym Fitness design

Audience: people exploring training, including first-time gym visitors. The page should help them choose a focus and start a conversation with the owner.

Palette: evergreen #174C3C, sage #E8F1EA, white #FFFFFF, graphite #22352C, lemon #E2EB83, muted green #58675F. Display: Barlow Condensed 800; body and controls: Manrope. Fonts are self-hosted with their OFL licenses.

Layout: a light, generous club identity. Rounded split hero, large athlete photography, three distinct training pathways, then a coaching story, questions, and a simple enquiry flow. Text remains left aligned. The movement names from the original website live inside the appropriate training details rather than overwhelming the home page.

Desktop: wordmark / navigation / enquiry → split hero → training in three columns → photo and coaching steps → FAQ and enquiry → social footer.
Mobile: compact wordmark / menu → headline / actions / photo → stacked training → coaching → FAQ → enquiry. Details open at the top in a full-screen sheet with a sticky close button.

Review: the previous redesign used navy and cobalt. This page uses a light green sports-club identity. Remove decorative counters, unverified testimonials, invented trainer identities, pricing and schedules. Use the repository's original photos, with honest generic alt text where the original filenames mislabeled an exercise. Restrained motion is limited to control feedback.

Contact: there is no working form endpoint in the source. Prepare an editable message locally and let the visitor copy it before opening the owner's supplied social profile. Never claim the message was sent or a session booked.
